Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to questions about our WhatsApp Alerts service.

How does the 3-day free trial work?
When you enter your number above and confirm your PIN, your 3-day free trial starts immediately. You will receive 2 to 3 curated live WhatsApp job alerts daily matching your job role and location preferences for 3 full days with no payment details required. When you are ready for full real-time dispatches (up to 7 alerts daily), you can upgrade to any VIP tier.
Can I update my role or location preferences later?
Yes. You can modify your target job title, location, or minimum salary at any time directly through your candidate portal at jobyuk.com/my-alerts.
How do I pause alerts if I find a job?
You can pause alerts with 1 tap from your candidate dashboard, or simply reply PAUSE to any message from our WhatsApp gateway. You can unpause whenever you resume your job search.
What payment options are supported?
We accept major Credit & Debit Cards (Apple Pay and Google Pay enabled), PayPal, Binance USDT (TRC-20 & BEP-20), as well as direct UK Bank Transfer / Wise Pro-Forma Invoices via our billing concierge.
