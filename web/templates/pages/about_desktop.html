Our Mission: Eliminating Job Board Noise

We built JobyUK.com because we were tired of clicking on vacancies that had already been filled, getting bounced between deceptive aggregator sites, and seeing the exact same recycled ghost postings everywhere.

We pull vacancies straight from verified employer hiring portals and official applicant tracking systems (including Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, SmartRecruiters, and Reed ATS feeds). If a role is filled or closed by the employer, our system automatically removes it. No dead ends. No runaround.

Data Sourcing & Quality Standards

In strict compliance with Google Search Essentials and People-First Content standards, our indexing pipeline enforces rigorous quality parameters:

  • Verified Source Feeds: Every opportunity originates from authenticated employer career feeds — never scraped third-party spam boards.
  • Strict Deduplication: Advanced fingerprint matching ensures each unique vacancy appears only once.
  • Active Freshness Check: Roles older than 30 days are automatically archived.
  • UK Exclusivity: Every vacancy is strictly verified for England, Scotland, Wales, or Northern Ireland work eligibility.
